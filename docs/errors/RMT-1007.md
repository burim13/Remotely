# RMT-1007: The PC could not be reached

Shown by: the Remotely app and the Server Manager.

## What it means

The PC was found (or its address typed) but a connection to it could not be opened: it is off, asleep, on another network, or a firewall blocks the port.

## What to do

Check that the other PC is on and sharing and on the same network. On that PC, check that the Windows Firewall rules "Remotely same-network (TCP)" and "(UDP)" exist and are enabled.
