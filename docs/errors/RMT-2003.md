# RMT-2003: A signature did not check out

Shown by: the Remotely app.

## What it means

The other side's signature is not valid for its key. Data was changed on the way, or the other side is not the device it claims to be.

## What to do

Do not continue on a network you do not trust. Try again; if it repeats, add the device again.
