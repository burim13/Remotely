# RMT-2004: This device is not trusted by the other PC

Shown by: the Remotely app.

## What it means

The PC you connect to does not have this device on its list of devices allowed to connect.

## What to do

On the other PC, allow this device, or pair this device with the same server.

See also: [troubleshooting](../troubleshooting.md), [all error codes](README.md).
