# RMT-3010: That device is offline

Shown by: the Remotely app.

## What it means

The device you want is not connected to the server right now (switched off, asleep, no internet, or its Remotely is not running).

## What to do

Turn the device on and wait until it shows as online, then try again.

See also: [troubleshooting](../troubleshooting.md), [all error codes](README.md).
