# RMT-1010: The saved PC did not accept this PC

Shown by: the Remotely app.

## What it means

The PC at the saved address no longer lists this PC as allowed, is not sharing, or a different PC now has that address.

## What to do

Connect once with a token from that PC and allow this PC again when asked.
